🏠 Safety Center

Your safety and security are our top priorities. Learn how to protect yourself from scams and stay safe when using our Platform.

🚨 Common Scams to Avoid

1. Fake Listings

What to watch for:

  • Prices that seem too good to be true (e.g., luxury apartment for UGX 200,000/month)
  • Property photos that look like stock images
  • Extremely vague descriptions or missing address details
  • Agent refuses to meet in person or show the property

2. Advance Fee Fraud

What to watch for:

  • Request for payment before viewing the property
  • "Holding fee" or "reservation fee" without a written agreement
  • Pressure to pay quickly because "others are interested"
  • Requests for payment via untraceable methods

3. Fake Landlords

What to watch for:

  • Person claiming to be landlord but can't provide proof of ownership
  • Refuses to show title deed or land ownership documents
  • Requests payment in cash to a personal account (not company account)
  • Keys don't work or property is occupied by someone else

4. Phishing Attempts

What to watch for:

  • Emails claiming to be from Metro Care asking for your password
  • Links to fake login pages that look like our website
  • Unexpected requests for payment or personal information
  • Poor grammar and suspicious sender email addresses

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

Before Paying Anything

  • ✅ Always view the property in person (or have someone you trust view it)
  • ✅ Verify the agent or landlord's identity
  • ✅ Request to see ownership documents (Title Deed, Land Registration)
  • ✅ Ask for a written agreement/contract before making payments
  • ✅ Never pay for a property you haven't seen

Safe Payment Practices

  • ✅ Always get a receipt for any payment
  • ✅ Use traceable payment methods (bank transfer, mobile money with receipt)
  • ✅ Never send money via Western Union or money gram to someone you haven't met
  • ✅ Verify that the agent's name matches the account you're paying to
  • ✅ Report any suspicious payment requests to us immediately

Communication Safety

  • ✅ Keep all communication on our Platform when possible
  • ✅ Be wary of users who try to take conversations to WhatsApp immediately
  • ✅ Report any harassment or inappropriate messages
  • ✅ Never share your password or verification codes

🚨 What to Do If You Suspect a Scam

Immediate Steps:

  1. Stop all communication with the suspected scammer
  2. Do not send any more money
  3. Gather evidence: Save screenshots, messages, receipts, and contact information
  4. Report to Metro Care: Use our complaint system or email safety@metrocareproperties.ug
  5. File a police report: Report to your local police station or the Criminal Investigations Department (CID)
