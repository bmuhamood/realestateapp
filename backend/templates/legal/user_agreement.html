User Agreement

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

1. Agreement Overview

This User Agreement ("Agreement") is a binding contract between you ("User," "you," or "your") and Metro Care Properties ("we," "our," or "us"). By creating an account or using our Platform, you agree to be bound by this Agreement.

2. Eligibility

To use our Platform, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years old
  • Have the legal capacity to enter into binding contracts
  • Not be prohibited from using our Platform by law
  • Provide accurate and complete registration information

3. Account Responsibilities

As a user, you agree to:

  • Maintain the confidentiality of your password
  • Notify us immediately of unauthorized account access
  • Accept responsibility for all activities under your account
  • Not share your account with others
  • Not create multiple accounts without permission

4. Agent Responsibilities

If you register as an agent, you additionally agree to:

  • Provide proof of real estate license or certification
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date listings
  • Respond to inquiries within 24 hours
  • Honor published prices and terms
  • Comply with all applicable real estate laws in Uganda
  • Pay applicable fees (listings, boosts, subscriptions)
  • Not post misleading or fraudulent listings

5. Service Provider Responsibilities

If you register as a service provider, you additionally agree to:

  • Provide proof of business registration or professional certification
  • Deliver services as described in your listing
  • Honor quoted prices and timeframes
  • Maintain appropriate licenses and insurance
  • Respond to booking requests promptly
  • Pay applicable service fees

6. Booking Terms

When you book a property viewing through our Platform:

  • You agree to pay the booking fee (if applicable)
  • You will receive confirmation via email and Platform notification
  • You may cancel according to our cancellation policy
  • You agree to behave respectfully during property visits
  • You will not share booking details with unauthorized parties

7. Payment Terms

7.1 Fees

  • Booking Fees: Paid by users to confirm viewing appointments
  • Boost Packages: Paid by agents to promote property listings
  • Subscription Fees: Optional premium features (future)
  • Transaction Fees: May apply to certain services

7.2 Payment Processing

  • Payments are processed through third-party providers (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, etc.)
  • You authorize us to charge the payment method you provide
  • All fees are in Ugandan Shillings (UGX) unless otherwise stated
  • Fees are non-refundable except as explicitly stated in our refund policy

8. Cancellation and Refund Policy

8.1 Booking Cancellations

  • 24+ hours before viewing: Full refund of booking fee
  • Less than 24 hours: No refund
  • No-show at viewing: No refund
  • Cancellation by agent: Full refund to user

8.2 Boost Package Cancellations

  • No refunds for boost packages once activated
  • Technical issues reviewed case-by-case by support team

8.3 Refund Process

  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method
  • Refunds may take 3-7 business days to appear
  • Contact support for refund assistance

9. Prohibited Conduct

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Posting fake or fraudulent property listings
  • Harassing, threatening, or abusing other users
  • Using the Platform for illegal activities
  • Circumventing our fee structure
  • Attempting to hack or disrupt Platform operations
  • Scraping or copying content without permission
  • Creating multiple accounts to bypass restrictions
  • Impersonating another person or entity
  • Posting spam or irrelevant content
  • Attempting to take transactions off-platform to avoid fees

10. Enforcement

Violation of this Agreement may result in:

  • Warning or suspension of account privileges
  • Removal of listings or content
  • Temporary or permanent account suspension
  • Forfeiture of fees paid
  • Legal action in appropriate cases
  • Reporting to law enforcement authorities

11. Dispute Resolution

11.1 Internal Resolution

Before initiating legal action, you agree to attempt to resolve disputes through our complaints system. We will attempt to mediate disputes between users in good faith.

11.2 Mediation

If internal resolution fails, you agree to attempt mediation through a mutually agreed mediator in Kampala, Uganda, before arbitration or litigation.

11.3 Arbitration

Disputes that cannot be resolved through mediation shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Kampala, Uganda, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, Cap 4 Laws of Uganda.

11.4 Exceptions

The following may be brought in court without prior arbitration:

  • Intellectual property infringement claims
  • Fraud or criminal activity claims
  • Claims seeking injunctive relief

12. Termination

We may terminate or suspend your account immediately without notice for:

  • Violation of this Agreement
  • Fraudulent or illegal activity
  • Non-payment of fees
  • Harassment of other users
  • At our sole discretion

Upon termination, you lose access to your account and any associated data. Fees paid are non-refundable.

13. Survival

Sections regarding intellectual property, indemnification, limitation of liability, and dispute resolution survive termination of this Agreement.

14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, constitutes the entire agreement between you and Metro Care Properties regarding use of the Platform.

15. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

16. Waiver

Our failure to enforce any provision does not waive our right to enforce it later.

17. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda.

18. Changes to This Agreement

We may modify this Agreement at any time. Material changes will be notified via email or Platform notification. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance.

19. Contact

For questions about this User Agreement, contact us at legal@metrocareproperties.ug.
