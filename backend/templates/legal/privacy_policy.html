Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Metro Care Properties ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Platform. Please read this policy carefully. By using our Platform, you consent to our data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Register an account: Name, email address, phone number, username, password
  • Create a profile: Profile picture, bio, location, district, city
  • List properties: Property details, photos, videos, pricing, address
  • Book viewings: Preferred dates, messages, contact information
  • Make payments: Payment method, transaction details (we do not store full payment card details)
  • Submit verification documents: National ID, passport, business registration
  • Contact us: Email messages, support requests

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, search queries, features used
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Social Media: If you log in via Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and profile picture
  • Payment Processors: Transaction confirmation, but not full card details
  • Cloudinary: Image and document storage

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide Services: Enable property listings, searches, bookings, and communications
  • Personalize Experience: Show relevant properties, recommendations, and content
  • Process Transactions: Handle payments for bookings and boost packages
  • Verify Identity: Authenticate users and prevent fraud
  • Communicate: Send notifications, updates, and marketing (with consent)
  • Improve Platform: Analyze usage, fix bugs, and enhance features
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Enforce Terms: Detect and prevent prohibited activities

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

For users in the European Economic Area, we process your information based on:

  • Contract Performance: To provide services you requested
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our Platform and prevent fraud
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with laws and regulations
  • Consent: For marketing and optional data collection

5. How We Share Your Information

5.1 Sharing with Other Users

  • Agents: Your profile information is visible to users when you list properties
  • Buyers/Tenants: When you book a viewing, agents see your name and contact
  • Property Owners: Your information is shared when you communicate about properties

5.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who help us operate:

  • Cloudinary: Image and document storage
  • Payment Processors: MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Flutterwave
  • Hosting: Render (servers and database)
  • Analytics: Platform usage analysis (Google Analytics, etc.)

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, including to:

  • Comply with Ugandan laws and regulations
  • Respond to lawful requests from Uganda Communications Commission (UCC)
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Investigate fraud or illegal activities

5.4 Business Transfers

If we are acquired or merge with another company, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. We may retain and use your information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data (passwords, payment information)
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments
  • Employee training on data protection

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Account Information

You can update or correct your account information at any time through your profile settings.

8.2 Access and Portability

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

8.3 Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal data. We will retain data required for legal or legitimate business purposes.

8.4 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of marketing emails by clicking "unsubscribe" or updating your notification preferences.

8.5 Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Uganda where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

11. Uganda Data Protection Compliance

We comply with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. This includes:

  • Registering as a data controller with NITA-Uganda
  • Obtaining consent before collecting sensitive data
  • Notifying users of data collection purposes
  • Providing data access and correction rights
  • Implementing security measures to protect data

12. Third-Party Links

Our Platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or Platform notification. The "Last Updated" date indicates when changes were made.

15. Complaints

If you believe we have violated your privacy rights, you have the right to lodge a complaint with:

  • NITA-Uganda: National Information Technology Authority
  • Personal Data Protection Office: Uganda's data protection supervisory authority
