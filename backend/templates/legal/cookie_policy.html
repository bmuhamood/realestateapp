🍪 Cookie Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our Platform. They help us remember your preferences, analyze usage, and improve your experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for the Platform to function properly:

  • Authentication cookies (keep you logged in)
  • Security cookies (prevent fraud)
  • Session cookies (remember your shopping cart or booking)

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings:

  • Language preferences
  • Search filters and sorting preferences
  • Saved properties

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with our Platform:

  • Page views and navigation paths
  • Time spent on pages
  • Search queries and property interactions
  • Performance monitoring

Marketing Cookies

Used to show relevant advertisements (only with your consent):

  • Property recommendations based on your browsing
  • Retargeting ads on other websites
  • Measuring ad campaign effectiveness

Third-Party Cookies

We use services that set their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Platform analytics
  • Cloudinary: Image and video delivery
  • Social Login: Google and Facebook authentication
  • Payment Processors: MTN, Airtel, Flutterwave

We do not control third-party cookies. Please review their privacy policies for more information.

Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

Disabling cookies may affect Platform functionality (e.g., staying logged in, saved preferences).

Cookie Consent

When you first visit our Platform, you'll see a cookie banner. By continuing to use our Platform, you consent to our use of essential and preference cookies. For analytics and marketing cookies, we will request your explicit consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
