Data Protection Compliance

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

Our Commitment to Data Protection

Metro Care Properties is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (the "Act"). This page explains how we comply with the Act and your rights under Ugandan law.

Our Registration

Metro Care Properties is registered as a Data Controller with the National Information Technology Authority - Uganda (NITA-Uganda). Our registration number is available upon request.

Personal Data We Process

We process the following categories of personal data in compliance with the Act:

  • Identity Data: Name, username, profile picture
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, physical address
  • Verification Data: National ID, passport, business registration (with consent)
  • Transaction Data: Payment records, booking history, property interactions
  • Technical Data: IP address, device information, usage analytics
  • Location Data: District, city, approximate location (with consent)

Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the Act, we process personal data based on:

  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent (e.g., marketing emails, verification documents)
  • Contract Performance: To provide services you requested (e.g., property listings, bookings)
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with laws and regulations
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our Platform and prevent fraud

Data Collection

We collect personal data directly from you when you:

  • Create an account
  • List a property
  • Book a viewing
  • Submit verification documents
  • Contact support
  • Subscribe to newsletters

We also collect data automatically through cookies and analytics tools.

Data Use

We use personal data for:

  • Providing and improving Platform services
  • Processing transactions and payments
  • Verifying user identities (with consent)
  • Preventing fraud and enforcing our terms
  • Communicating important updates
  • Analyzing usage and improving user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We share personal data with:

  • Service Providers: Cloudinary (image storage), Payment Processors (MTN, Airtel)
  • Other Users: As necessary for transactions (e.g., agents see your contact when you book)
  • Law Enforcement: When required by law or court order

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account closure, we retain data required for legal or legitimate business purposes for up to 5 years, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data (passwords, payment information)
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments and audits
  • Employee training on data protection

Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, we will:

  1. Investigate the breach immediately
  2. Notify affected users within 72 hours
  3. Notify NITA-Uganda as required by the Act
  4. Take corrective actions to prevent future breaches

Your Rights Under the Act

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

You may request a copy of your personal data we hold. We will provide this within 30 days, free of charge for the first request.

Right to Rectification

You may correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data through your profile settings or by contacting us.

Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

You may request deletion of your personal data when:

  • The data is no longer necessary for the purpose collected
  • You withdraw consent and no other legal basis applies
  • You object to processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds
  • The data has been unlawfully processed

Right to Restrict Processing

You may request restriction of processing while we verify accuracy or lawful basis.

Right to Data Portability

You may request your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format for transfer to another service provider.

Right to Object

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent for processing at any time where consent is the legal basis.

Consent Management

We obtain explicit consent for:

  • Processing verification documents (National ID, passport)
  • Marketing communications
  • Non-essential cookies
  • Location tracking (when enabled)

You can withdraw consent at any time through your account settings or by contacting us.

Children's Data

Our Platform is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us to delete it.

Cross-Border Data Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to countries outside Uganda (e.g., to our service providers). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard contractual clauses approved by NITA-Uganda
  • Binding corporate rules for group transfers
  • Transfers only to countries with adequate data protection laws

Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making that significantly affects you. Our recommendations are based on browsing history but do not constitute automated decisions.
